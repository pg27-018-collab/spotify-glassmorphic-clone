Catalog Integrations

Connect your Spotify Developer accounts to load playlists, searches, and albums directly from Spotify's database. If empty, the app automatically falls back to our free iTunes catalog.

How to get free credentials:
1. Open Spotify Developer Portal.
2. Create an App, set the redirect URI to http://localhost:8000.
3. Copy your Client ID & Client Secret and paste them here!
